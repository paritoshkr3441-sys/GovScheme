ABOUT GOVSCHEME

Making government support easier to discover.

GovScheme is a student project designed to bring information about scholarships, farmer support, employment, business, pensions, healthcare, housing and other welfare opportunities into one place.

Instead of searching many websites from scratch, users can start with their state and situation, explore possible options, and visit the relevant official portal for details.

Important: This is an independent discovery tool, not a government website. Scheme information, eligibility, benefits, deadlines and application links can change. Always verify details with the responsible government department before applying.
